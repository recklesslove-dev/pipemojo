Questions

How do I get on the board?

Play a round. In Whack-a-Pipe, claim a name on the Cursed screen when the round ends. In Flow, claim one in the leaderboard section under the board. In Mojo Roll, open the menu and tap Rank & Board. In Hot & Cold, claim one in the leaderboard section under the board. Your score posts right away, and every game after that posts on its own.

Is there a password?

No passwords and no email. When you claim a name, you get a one-time recovery code. Save it in Notes. It's the only way to sign back in to your name, and nobody can look it up for you, not even us.

How do I play on another phone or iPad?

Open either game, tap Already have a name? Use your recovery code, and enter your name and code. Your scores follow you, and so does your Flow level.

I lost my recovery code.

If you're still signed in on any device, tap New recovery code next to your name under either game's leaderboard. You'll get a fresh code, and the old one stops working. If you're signed out everywhere, that name can't be recovered, so claim a new one.

Can I change my name?

Not right now. You can tap Switch player and claim a new name, but your old scores stay under the old one.

Why didn't my score post?

You need to be signed in to a name, and you need a connection. Whack-a-Pipe scores are also checked against how long the round actually lasted, so fake scores get turned away.

What's the insignia next to a name?

That's the player's rank. Flow players are officers: every boss beaten (every 100 levels) is a promotion, from Second Lieutenant up to five-star General of the Army. Mojo Roll players are warrant officers: every 24 wrenches is a promotion, from Warrant Officer 1 up to Chief Warrant Officer 5. Whack-a-Pipe players are enlisted: every 100 points of their best score is a promotion, from Private up to Sergeant Major of the Army. Hot & Cold players go by the trade: every inspection passed (every 25 jobs) is a promotion, from Helper through Apprentice, Journeyman, Foreman and Superintendent up to Master Plumber and Plumbing Contractor. After the top rank come Silver, Gold and Diamond finishes. Tap or hover an insignia to see the rank.

What's the 1K badge?

The 1K Club. It's only for Flow players who have cleared level 1000 and Hot & Cold players who have passed job 1000. Clear 2000 and it becomes 2K, and so on.

What's the 🔥 next to a name?

That's the player's best par streak in Flow or Hot & Cold: the most levels in a row they've cleared on par or better. It's their best ever, so it stays even after a streak ends.

Does Flow save my progress?

Yes. Every move saves on your device. If you're signed in, your run also saves to your name each time you clear a level or leave the game, so you can pick it up on another device.
