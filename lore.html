Origins
New Orleans · 1847

Nobody remembers who stitched him together. Burlap. Twine. Two button eyes marked with an X. A tiny top hat someone thought was funny at the time. He wasn't made for anything grand — just meant to sit on a shelf and watch over a family that eventually moved on without him.

So he waited. For a long time.

✦
The Storm

Then came the storm. The kind New Orleans does better than anywhere else on earth. The flood took him under — through drains, tunnels, and passages the city above had long forgotten. When the water finally settled he was somewhere deep beneath the streets.

For the first time in his existence — he moved.

✦
The Work

Turns out the city below has problems. Blockages. Root intrusions. Buildup nobody wants to talk about. He started fixing things. Not because anyone asked. Not because anyone knew he existed. Just because the flow was off and somebody had to deal with it.

That somebody became him.

✦
About Ganja

The roach found him first. Back in the old house. Never afraid. Never left. Whatever strange energy lives beneath New Orleans changed them both over the years. Ganja's been around longer than any roach has a right to be.

Pipe Mojo talks. Ganja doesn't. He doesn't need to. That look he gives says plenty.

✦
The Legend

The workers who catch glimpses don't talk about it much. Just whispers between night crews and maintenance guys. A small figure in a top hat. A wrench that glows sometimes. A roach that knows where trouble is before trouble knows it's there.

Most people never see them.
