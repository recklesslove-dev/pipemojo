HOW TO ROLL

MOJO ROLL

  • 👆Drag or swipe anywhere on the board. The pipe leans the way it's about to go. Let go to roll, or slide back to cancel.
  • ◀Or use the thumb buttons: ◀ ▶ on the left, ▲ ▼ on the right. Up rolls away from you, down rolls toward you. Arrow keys work too.
  • ⭕Stand the pipe straight up in the flange to clear the level.
  • 🔧Hit par for 3 wrenches. Every zone brings a new twist.
  • ☰The menu has levels, sound, and full screen (iPad). On iPhone, use Share → Add to Home Screen to hide Safari's bars.
